# Manifest
I set sail upon this ship not of iron and wood, but of silicon and copper. My destination is not treasure made of gold or diamond, but the immaterial; Hark, for in the distance, beyond the fog of the unknown, I see an island. The island has many scrolls, each spoken through a tongue, something ancient, and at the same time, something new. The scrolls are beasts to be tamed, yet neither sword nor shield nor spear will put them down. The weapon of choice must be something deeper, more ancient, more unseen - that sea of consciousness. It creates dreams, and through dreams it creates worlds. It creates worlds, and through worlds it creates dreams.\
The sea I see before me, and the dream contained within, reminds me so dearly of Griffith's speech about dreams. That dreams can be like great storms, sweeping along a country with it. That they breathe life into men, but can cage them in suffering. That some men see nothing more than life or death; They are dead, for they have no dreams.\
But right here, right now, I am not griffith, for the world I see, and the dreams contained within, are by no means clear, and by no means exact. There is no country I wish to build, no throne I wish to sit upon. Rather, my dream is to have the power to do so. Between the Judge, who sees only war, and the White Hawk, who can only see beyond it, there is a piercing clarity that it is all a big game. A game unlike any other - none has so much to gain, and so much to lose. And yet, hold tight the sword and spear with which you rend, you carve the world into your dream, and the sword, the spaer, will betray you. A fighter must be loose at times, and firm at others, and so to must you see war, and the things beyond it. For you are not a demon, not a hero, not an angel, not a caricature. You are, in many ways, a man running away from a storm, a man who is yet to realize that the storm is within him.\
